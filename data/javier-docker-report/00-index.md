# Javier Alonso — Impact at Docker

*A record of meaningful work, built from Jira, Notion, Slack, Gmail/Calendar, Granola meeting notes, Google Drive, and Sigma analytics. Metrics current to 22 Jul 2026.*

**Role:** Staff Product Designer (principal-designer track), Product Design team · at Docker since Jan 2023 · based in Madrid.

Over three-plus years Javier moved from Docker Desktop/CLI UX into the lead design role for Docker's entire AI product line — **Docker Model Runner, Gordon, the MCP Toolkit & Catalog, and the Agentic Platform / AI Cloud** — while operating as a rare designer-builder who also *runs his own user research* and ships working prototypes and internal tools. The surfaces he designed now account for tens of millions of usage events and hundreds of thousands of users.

---

## One-line impact

- **Gordon** grew from **69 → ~119,700 monthly active users** (Nov 2024 → Jun 2026) — a product he designed 0→1.
- **Docker Model Runner:** ~2.1M model pulls, ~91K users — the local-AI foundation he designed 0→1.
- **MCP Catalog & Toolkit:** ~13.1M catalog pulls, ~10.6M tool-call events; and his UX iterations **more than doubled Toolkit activation** between Desktop 4.45 → 4.46.
- Built **Docker's first CLI Design System**, now an org-wide standard.
- Runs his own **UX research program** (interviews, surveys, usability tests) and is the bylined author on multiple research reports.
