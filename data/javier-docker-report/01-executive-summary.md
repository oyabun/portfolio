# 1. Executive summary

← [Back to index](00-index.md)

Javier Alonso is the design lead behind Docker's push into AI. Across 2023–2026 he owned the UX for the four surfaces that now define Docker's AI story, and — unusually for a designer — he pairs that with a running user-research practice and the habit of shipping his own working prototypes and tools.

## What he is known for

**Designer-builder.** He doesn't stop at Figma. He ships deployed HTML/Vercel prototypes tied directly to GitHub issues/PRs (`docker/design-ja-*` repos) and has built two working macOS apps (Den, Vein). Colleagues note his tools preview where the official product is heading.

**Researcher.** He authors his own research plans, writes discussion guides and survey instruments, facilitates usability sessions, and synthesizes findings — for Model Runner, Gordon, the CLI, and the Agentic Platform. He is the bylined author/co-author on several formal research reports (see [page 10](10-research-practice.md)).

**0→1 operator.** Model Runner, Gordon, the MCP Toolkit/Catalog, the Agentic Platform, the CLI Design System, and Den were all net-new — designed from scratch (see [page 4](04-zero-to-one.md)).

## Main successes

- Took **Gordon** from a 69-user prototype (Nov 2024) to **~119,700 monthly active users** (Jun 2026) and **~4.3M requests/month**, through GA (Desktop 4.74, May 2026) and monetization (V2 GA + pricing, Apr 2026).
- Designed **Docker Model Runner** end-to-end — now **~2.1M model pulls** across **~91K users** and **~124K installs**.
- Designed the **MCP surfaces** that made Docker a hub for agent tooling — **~13.1M catalog pulls**, **~10.6M tool-call events** — and drove UX iterations that **~2× the Toolkit's activation rate** from Desktop 4.45 → 4.46.
- Created **Docker's first CLI Design System**, adopted org-wide (e.g., Docker Offload).
- Recognized as a thought leader: the "genuinely novel" `--help-agent` concept, early Figma-MCP power user courted by Figma for **Config 2026**, and on the **principal-designer track**.

## Scope of ownership

Embedded across Agentic Platform weekly planning, AI Cloud UX journey reviews, Gordon UX reviews, and IC design syncs, with 1:1s spanning design leadership (Roberta Carraro), management (Arnaud Héritier, Earl Carlson), and product/eng leaders — a reach well beyond a single product team.
