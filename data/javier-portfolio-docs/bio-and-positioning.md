## Long bio (120 words)
Javier Alonso is a principal / head-of-design–level product designer specializing in 0→1 AI and developer experiences. Over three-plus years at Docker he became the design lead for the company's AI line: **Gordon** (grown from 69 to ~119,700 monthly active users), **Docker Model Runner** (~2.1M model pulls), the **MCP Toolkit & Catalog** (~13M+ pulls, with activation roughly doubled across two releases), and the **Agentic Platform / AI Cloud**, whose primitives he defined. He is unusual in combining product design with a hands-on user-research practice — authoring research plans, facilitating studies, and building feedback pipelines — and an engineering-grade prototyping habit, shipping working HTML/Vercel prototypes and his own macOS tools. He also created Docker's first CLI Design System, now an organization-wide standard.
