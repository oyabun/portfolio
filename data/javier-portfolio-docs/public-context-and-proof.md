## The "public number" for each story
- **Gordon:** Docker publicly reported a **9× increase in weekly active users**. *(Internally the growth was larger — 69 → ~119.7K MAU — but the 9× figure is safe to quote.)* GA'd **May 19, 2026**, built into Desktop 4.74+ and the CLI, free with every account.
- **Docker Model Runner:** **Beta Apr 2025 → GA Sep 18, 2025**; open source (`github.com/docker/model-runner`); the GA post highlights the **"guided onboarding to help even first-time AI developers start serving models"** — the UX Javier designed.
- **MCP Catalog & Toolkit:** launched **May 2025 with 100+ verified tools** from **Stripe, Elastic, Neo4j, Grafana, Pulumi, Heroku, Kong, New Relic, Continue.dev**; one-click connect to Gordon, Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Goose.
- **Docker scale for context:** **20M+ developers, billions of image pulls/month.**

## Proof the safety UX is the product's headline
Docker's own Gordon post describes the permission model exactly as Javier designed it:
> "Gordon shows you what it wants to do, you approve or reject, then it executes. Every command. Every file update. Every Docker operation."
Use this to show your design became the product's core trust mechanic.

## Corroboration of authorship
- The **Model Runner GA** post is co-authored by **Francesco Corti** — Javier's co-author on the internal "Docker Model Runner – Insights" research. Independent confirmation he was in the room on DMR.
