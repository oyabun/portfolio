Positioning

A design leader who ships, researches, and moves metrics

Over three-plus years at Docker I grew from Desktop/CLI UX into the lead designer for the company's AI line. Unusually, I pair product design with a hands-on user-research practice and an engineering-grade prototyping habit — I ship working code, run my own studies, and built Docker's first CLI Design System, now an org-wide standard.
