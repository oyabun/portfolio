About 序

The hand at the press

Javier Alonso is a principal-level product designer leading design for Docker’s AI line. He grew Gordon from a 69-user prototype to ~119,700 monthly active users and designed surfaces serving ~13M MCP pulls; he pairs product design with a hands-on research practice and engineering-grade prototyping — he ships working HTML/Swift, and his macOS app Den is live on the App Store.
