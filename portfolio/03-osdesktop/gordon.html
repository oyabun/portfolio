Gordon

From a 69-user prototype to ~119,700 monthly active users.

Docker · Design lead, end-to-end · 2024–2026

Personas: Ana (AI-curious dev) · Sam (newcomer) · Marco (safety & governance) · Dana (plans & pricing)

Context

Docker needed an AI assistant meeting developers where they work — Desktop + CLI — no separate chat tool, and no unsafe agent actions on their machine. A true 0→1 bet: no established pattern, no existing users.

Role

Interaction model · onboarding · safety/permission UX · billing & GA flows · support integration · the feedback research pipeline.

Jobs-to-be-done

  • “Ask about my containers/images/compose without leaving Docker.” Gordon in global search + Containers/Images/Volumes tabs.
  • “Let the agent act — but never do something destructive without my okay.” Danger classification (cautious/safer tiers), allow/deny dialog, CLI hook. The safety pattern became the reference for other agent dialogs across Docker.
  • “Understand what Gordon costs and which plan I need.” Budget/token views, plan mode, pricing.
  • “Get started without reading docs.” Onboarding + login nudge.
  • “Tell Docker when Gordon gets it wrong.” In-product Qualtrics feedback → permalink → Slack routing.

Process

Authored the continuous research plan; stood up the feedback pipeline; iterated free/blocked states, JIT nudges, permission tiers across releases; shipped V2 GA + pricing (Apr 2026), GA with Desktop 4.74 (May 2026), again in 4.80 (Jul 2026).

Impact

Reflection

Proudest of the safety UX — it made “let the agent act” shippable, and propagated to other products.
